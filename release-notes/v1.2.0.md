ArkStore is now on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux: an app store for apps that live on GitHub.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi** if you prefer MSI). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian.

### New
- ArkStore for Windows, macOS and Linux: each shows apps for that platform and installs the right build for your computer (installer on Windows, app into Applications on Mac, AppImage or package on Linux), then keeps them updated
- Developers can list desktop apps: a GitHub release with an EXE, MSI, DMG, AppImage or DEB is enough, with or without an APK
- Account lists every device signed in to your account, on any platform, and can sign any of them out
- Get ArkStore page: recognizes your device and puts the right download first
- ArkStore updates itself, on desktop and Android

### Heads up
- The desktop builds aren't code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen may ask you to confirm (More info, Run anyway), and on a Mac right-click ArkStore and choose Open the first time
