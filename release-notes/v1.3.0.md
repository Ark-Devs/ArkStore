ArkStore on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and iPad.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi**). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian. iPhone and iPad: set up from ArkStore on your computer (Account › Set up iPhone).

**Android: reinstall once.** From this version ArkStore for Android is signed with its own release key, so it can't update over 1.2.x: uninstall ArkStore, then install this APK. From now on updates install over it and keep you signed in.

### New
- **iPhone apps without SideStore.** ArkStore on your computer installs ArkStore on your iPhone with your own free Apple Account: plug in the iPhone, sign in (the verification code goes right in ArkStore), click Install. On Windows it adds Apple's iPhone driver for you
- **Renews on the iPhone itself.** After that first install, ArkStore on the iPhone installs apps and renews them (and itself) before their 7 days run out, with LocalDevVPN connected. No computer needed again unless you reset the iPhone
- **Install on iPhone** from every iPhone app's page, on your computer or on the iPhone
- ArkStore on your computer also renews the iPhone apps it installed whenever the iPhone is plugged in or on the same Wi-Fi
- **Sign-in methods** under Account, ready for more ways to sign in. Publishing apps needs your GitHub account connected
- The download page lives at **store.arkdevs.xyz**

### Fixed
- Signing in no longer has to be repeated after Android updates (see above: one last reinstall)
