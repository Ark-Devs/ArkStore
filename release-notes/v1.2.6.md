ArkStore on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and iPad.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi**). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian. iPhone and iPad: the **ios.ipa**, installed with SideStore (see the guide on the Get ArkStore page).

### Fixed
- **Android: apps you uninstall no longer show as installed.** ArkStore checks the phone every time you come back to it
- **Android: apps installed outside ArkStore are recognized.** An app you got from GitHub, a browser or the Play Store now shows **Open** instead of Get
- The setup download button no longer overflows its box on phones

### New
- **ArkStore for iPhone and iPad**: the app itself, installed through SideStore, with reminders 3 days and 1 day before its signature runs out (tap to open SideStore and refresh)
- **One-click computer setup** for the SideStore install: a script for Windows, macOS and Linux on the Get ArkStore page
- **The ArkStore shortcut** for iPhone: pick an app from a list and install it with SideStore, plus a guide to refresh apps automatically with Shortcuts
- **Install with SideStore** button on every iPhone app page
- The iOS source now lists only apps SideStore can install (no TrollStore-only or Apple TV builds), fixing certificate errors
