ArkStore on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and iPad.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi**). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian. iPhone and iPad: set up from ArkStore on your computer (Account › Set up iPhone).

### iPhone
- **No more 3-app limit.** Tap **GET** and the app goes into LiveContainer, which runs apps inside itself, so a free Apple Account's 3-app and 10-App-ID limits don't apply. The first GET sets LiveContainer up for you: ArkStore installs it, gives it your signing certificate and keeps it renewed. Nothing else to install
- **Home Screen shortcuts:** in LiveContainer, hold an app and choose Add to Home Screen
- Installing an app as its own separate app is still there, on the app's iPhone page
- **"device socket io failed"** now says to connect LocalDevVPN
- ArkStore keeps updating itself on the iPhone, with LocalDevVPN connected

Apps inside LiveContainer can't use widgets, share extensions or push notifications. If you need those for an app, install it as its own app instead.

If ArkStore on your iPhone can't update itself yet, install **ArkStore-1.4.0-ios.ipa** the same way you installed it before.
