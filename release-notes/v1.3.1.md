ArkStore on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and iPad.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi**). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian. iPhone and iPad: set up from ArkStore on your computer (Account › Set up iPhone).

### iPhone
- **Get installs with ArkStore itself.** On an iPhone set up from a computer, tapping Get on an iPhone app opens ArkStore's own installer, signed with your Apple Account. SideStore, TrollStore and the IPA are still there under "Other ways to install"
- **Clear errors in iPhone setup.** Every error says what to do, keeps Apple's own message, and has Show details with the full error. It no longer says "Unlock the iPhone" for errors that have nothing to do with the iPhone
- When ArkStore on your computer can't see the iPhone, it shows why instead of only asking to plug it in

To get this on your iPhone: open ArkStore on your computer › Account › Set up iPhone and install ArkStore again (your apps and sign-in stay).
