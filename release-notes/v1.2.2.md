ArkStore on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi**). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian.

### New
- Studio lists the repos of your GitHub organizations, not just your own
- When you sign in, GitHub lets you Grant ArkStore access to organizations you own, or Request it from an organization's owners. Sign out and back in once to see it
- "Don't see your organization's repos?" in Studio opens that approval page directly
