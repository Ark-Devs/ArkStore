ArkStore on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux, and now iPhone and iPad apps.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi**). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian. iPhone and iPad: see below.

### New
- **iPhone and iPad apps**: open-source iOS apps from GitHub, installed through SideStore, AltStore or TrollStore. ArkStore publishes them as a source SideStore reads, so they install with one tap and show their updates
- **Step-by-step iPhone guide** on the Get ArkStore page: the one-time SideStore setup (the only part that needs a computer), adding ArkStore, keeping apps signed without the 7-day limit getting in the way, and fixes for common problems
- **Install on iPhone** page for every iOS app: Add to SideStore, Install with TrollStore, or download the IPA for your own signer
- **iPhone & iPad** in the platform switcher, and iOS apps shown first on iPhones
- More iOS apps found automatically, including from the community sources run by SideStore, UTM, Provenance and iSH
