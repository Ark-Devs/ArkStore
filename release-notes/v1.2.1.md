ArkStore on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi**). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian.

### New
- ArkStore tells you when it has a new version: a banner on every screen, a badge on Updates and a notification, with one tap to update
- The desktop app also checks for its own update when you come back to it

### Fixed
- Signing in from the website returns to the right page

If you're on Android 1.1.0, install this version once from the download page; after that ArkStore updates itself.
