ArkStore on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and iPad.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi**). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian. iPhone and iPad: set up from ArkStore on your computer (Account › Set up iPhone).

### iPhone
- **Installing on the iPhone no longer asks for a computer.** The computer's setup now goes inside ArkStore itself when it installs ArkStore on the iPhone, so Get › Install works right on the iPhone with LocalDevVPN connected. Before, the setup file could fail to reach the iPhone and ArkStore kept saying "Set up once with a computer"
- **Clearer iPhone setup screen:** what to do when setup is missing, and the real error (with Show details) when LocalDevVPN isn't reachable

### Android
- **Fewer Play Protect warnings:** ArkStore no longer asks for permissions it doesn't need

**Already have ArkStore on an iPhone?** Do this once: update ArkStore on your computer to 1.3.3, plug in the iPhone, Account › Set up iPhone › install ArkStore. Your apps and Apple Account stay. After that, installs, renewals and updates happen on the iPhone.
