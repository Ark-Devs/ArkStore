ArkStore on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and iPad.

**Which file?** Android: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every phone (not sure? take **universal**). Windows: the **windows-x64-setup.exe** (or the **.msi**). Mac: the **.dmg** for your chip (**arm64** for Apple silicon, **x64** for Intel). Linux: the **.AppImage**, or the **.deb** on Ubuntu / Debian. iPhone and iPad: set up from ArkStore on your computer (Account › Set up iPhone).

### Everywhere
- **Contact us** from Account: support@arkdevs.xyz for help, issues@arkdevs.xyz to report a problem, admin@arkdevs.xyz for app takedowns and abuse, contact@arkdevs.xyz for everything else

### iPhone
- **Use a pairing file you already have.** If ArkStore got onto your iPhone without ArkStore on a computer (for example with iloader or SideStore), Get no longer stops at "Set up once with a computer". Tap **Choose pairing file** and pick the one from iloader, SideStore's setup or JitterbugPair. ArkStore then signs and installs apps on the iPhone itself, with LocalDevVPN connected
- **Clearer LocalDevVPN step:** when ArkStore can't reach the iPhone, it now says why

If ArkStore on your iPhone can't install apps by itself yet, install **ArkStore-1.3.3-ios.ipa** the same way you installed it before, then choose your pairing file. After that, ArkStore updates itself on the iPhone.
